# Primary contribution decision

## Decision

**Gate: CONDITIONAL GO**

Proceed with one paper identity only:

> An evidence-backed systems-security study deriving and testing the accounting-integrity
> and solvency conditions of token-metered streaming inference across synchronization,
> terminal-path commitment, and usage authority.

The contribution is not a new concurrency primitive, a new trust-input principle, a
production prevalence study, a proof of the implementation, or a vulnerability finding
against any third party. B0 is calibration. M1's orthogonality and M2's
computation-versus-authority distinction are the scientific center. The two TLA+ models
provide bounded exhaustive evidence for different mechanisms.

## Conditions for GO at final review

1. Cite and contrast the September 2026 provider-side PTIA work and the 2026 pay-per-token
   auditing work.
2. Execute at least one preregistered decisive extension that can falsify a structural
   prediction, or leave it explicitly unexecuted and downgrade the submission verdict.
3. Independently reproduce all headline empirical and TLC numbers from raw inputs.
4. Replace broad “verified” wording with bounded/model-checked wording wherever the bound
   is not adjacent.
5. Fix anonymous source and rendered DOI leaks; confirm exclusivity and both authors'
   required submission metadata.

Failure of conditions 1, 3, or 4 is a `NO-GO`. Failure to execute a new experiment is not
misrepresented as success; it leaves the paper potentially submit-ready only if the
existing contribution survives the adversarial review without relying on the proposal.
