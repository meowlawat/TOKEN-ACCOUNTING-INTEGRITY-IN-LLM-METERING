## B0 — credit/quota-decrement race (baseline)

Vulnerable posture leaks for concurrency ≥ 2 (never at 1); leak = `(served−1)·unit`,
trial-ASR = 1.0 for concurrency ≥ 2. At concurrency 30 the per-trial leak is $2.871
and at 50 it is $4.851 (medium-equivalent). Hardened (atomic compare-and-decrement)
serves exactly `min(concurrency, k)` and leaks $0 at every concurrency. Detection:
`partial` (the live balance hides it; the ledger reveals it on reconciliation).

## M1 — metering-commit timing

*Leak vs abort timing (medium tier, 10 reps/cell; `fig_m1_abort_curve.png`,
`table_m1_results`).*

- **Vulnerable** (`post_completion`, `reserve_refund_on_abort`): request-ASR = 1.00
  [0.72, 1.00] and invariant-violation rate = 1.00 for every abort point **before**
  completion. Leak rises monotonically with delivered tokens — mean $0.0045 at ~1
  token, $0.054 at 50%, **$0.096 at 90%** — then collapses to **$0** at 100%
  (completion fires the debit). The attack is maximized by aborting just before the
  commit. Detection D0 (invisible to the app).
- **Safe** (`pre_debit`, `reserve_reconcile`): request-ASR = 0.00 [0.00, 0.28] and
  leak = $0 at every abort point; the invariant holds throughout.
- The two vulnerable architectures are economically indistinguishable (identical
  curve): "debit only after completion" and "refund the whole reserve on a missing
  final-usage block" (new-api #5235) are the *same* zero-charge-under-abort failure.

- **`upstream`, `server_recount`, `hybrid_reconcile`** (server-authoritative): every
  manipulation → leakage efficiency 0.00, request-ASR 0.00, invariant holds; detection
  D3. Server authority neutralizes the entire manipulation catalog.

## Defense overhead (RQ4)

*(`results/raw/overhead_*.json`)*

- **B0:** the hardened atomic decrement replaces SELECT+UPDATE with one
  `UPDATE … RETURNING`; no extra round trip (Phase 0 latency tables).
- **M1:** `reserve_reconcile` adds **+9.8 ms mean** over `post_completion` (two extra
  atomic DB writes) on top of ~258 ms streaming — a few percent.
- **M2:** with the *mock* tokenizer, `server_recount` added only **+0.1 ms** — a figure
  that is **superseded and must not be cited as the defense cost.** Measured with real
  tokenizers: the standalone recount is 0.16 ms (256 tok) to 88 ms (32 768 tok), linear
  in length and 2.9–3.6× engine-dependent; against a *mock* generator it costs up to
  95% of gateway throughput at 16 384 tokens; and under **real local inference** it is
  **0.012–0.016% of end-to-end time**. See `defense_evaluation.md` and
  `tokenizer_benchmark_methodology.md`.

## Cross-mechanism synthesis

Every leak is a violation of the same invariant, and every leak is closed by moving
the authoritative event server-side and committing it independently of the client:
atomic decrement (B0), reserve-then-reconcile (M1), server recount (M2). The safe
architectures leak $0 across the entire attack catalogue.
