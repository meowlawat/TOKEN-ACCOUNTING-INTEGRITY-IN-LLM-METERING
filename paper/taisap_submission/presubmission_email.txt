Subject: Pre-submission scope question -- AI-serving billing/metering security

Dear Editors,

I am preparing a submission to TAISAP titled "Accounting Integrity in Metered
LLM-Serving Infrastructure: Verified Conditions Against Client-Side Under-Payment."

Abstract: Metered AI-inference services are deployed systems in which computational
value is streamed to the client before the charge is settled, usage is produced at
runtime, and settlement is often asynchronous. We study the integrity of that boundary
against an authenticated client that seeks to pay less than it owes for inference it
received, modeling the lifecycle with a single integrity property, verified exhaustively
in TLA+/TLC and validated empirically across multiple serving architectures, including a
real LLM-serving stack. We find that correct usage computation does not imply correct
billing, and report the defenses that close the resulting leaks and their cost.

One question before I submit: is the security of the usage-metering and billing path in
AI-serving infrastructure -- a technical vulnerability of a deployed AI system, not a
model-level attack -- within TAISAP's scope?

Thank you for your time.
