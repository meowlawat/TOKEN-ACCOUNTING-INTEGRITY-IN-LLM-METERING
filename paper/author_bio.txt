

Hardik is an undergraduate researcher in Computer Science and Data Analytics at the
Indian Institute of Technology Patna, dual-enrolled in Computer Science and Engineering
(Cybersecurity) at the Vivekananda School of Engineering and Technology, New Delhi. His
work sits at the intersection of systems security and machine learning, with accepted
papers on trusted-execution-environment architectures for medical data sharing and on
LSTM-based password guessing under memory-hard hashing constraints. His current interests
are billing integrity, usage metering, and the formal specification of accounting
protocols for machine-learning inference services.
