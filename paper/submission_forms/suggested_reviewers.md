# Suggested reviewers

Editorial Manager asks for suggested reviewers at submission. These candidates are drawn
from the manuscript's own reference list — people who have published directly on LLM
billing integrity, usage accounting, or the race-condition and formal-methods work the
paper builds on.

**Contact details are deliberately left blank.** Names and affiliations change, and a
reviewer suggestion with a wrong or guessed email address is worse than no suggestion.
Take each person's current affiliation and correspondence address from the cited paper
itself, or from their institutional page, immediately before submitting.

**Conflicts:** none. This is a single-author manuscript with no prior collaboration,
co-authorship, shared affiliation, or supervisory relationship with any person below.

## A note on scope

If the handling editor is uncertain whether the paper is in scope, the cover letter's
first section addresses this directly: the security object is metering and accounting
infrastructure, not the language model. Reviewers whose expertise is adversarial ML or
model safety would be a poor fit — not because the work is weak there, but because it
makes no claims there at all.
